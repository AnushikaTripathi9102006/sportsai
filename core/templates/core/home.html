Challenges in Legacy Procurement

Traditional Procurement Can Be Difficult

Conventional procurement centers often suffer from severe operational bottlenecks, causing stress and loss for farmers.

❌

Long Waiting Times

Farmers spend hours or even days standing in unmanaged physical queues.

❌

Manual Processes

Handwritten logs and paper receipts lead to clerical errors and discrepancies.

❌

Unclear Status

Zero visibility into current queue length or center processing capacity.

❌

Multiple Visits

Traveling to distant centers without knowing if they accept your specific crop.

❌

Payment Uncertainty

Lack of real-time tracking on net weight calculations and payout timelines.
